風雨殿

Temple of Wind and Rain

A building played by weather. Wind through the walls, rain on glass, sun in the pitch. The building only tunes and listens.

The model follows the text.

i · Origin

A door in winter

Where it began: a gap that sang in the wind.

New York. A northwest door, a narrow gap, a hard wind. The gap sang. From across the room, it was almost music.

ii · Wall

The wall as instrument

A slot lets the wind speak. A chamber sets the pitch.

Wind brings little pressure, so the voices are large and low. Air passes through a slot and breaks on an edge; a tuned chamber behind it holds the pitch. Shutters close in a storm.

Show

At 15–40 km/h, wind supplies 10–74 Pa. Length and volume set pitch. An open pipe overblows to the octave, a stopped pipe to the twelfth, so gusts stay within the chord.

iii · Pedal

One held note

One low A beneath everything.

Every wind face shares a low A, at 55 and 110 Hz. It never moves. Everything else is colour above it.

Sun and resonance

The high A sounds from two matched chambers, one sunlit, one shaded. Warmth raises pitch about 3 cents per °C, so by day the pair beats slowly; at dusk it settles. Strings tuned to the A series line the listening ring and hum faintly with everything near them.

iv · Faces

Eight faces

Each season's wind opens a different face.

One face for each trigram. The prevailing wind turns with the year: 震 in spring, 巽 in summer, 兌 in autumn, 乾 in winter. Over the same A, each face sounds its own chord: summer cool and open, winter warm.

Live. Each line a sounding string, each mark a drop on glass. Sound drifts left as it fades.

The other four faces

離, south: light. 坤, southwest: entrance. 坎, north: water out. 艮, northeast: silence. Spring and autumn are piano-tuned and lean away from A, so they play softer. Summer and winter use the harmonic series and rest on it. Voicings are provisional.

Why it stays consonant

Roughness depends on the distance in hertz between two tones, and that distance is about the same in every register. Close intervals sit high or stay short. Low voices stay wide. Within that rule, any scale works above the pedal, microtones included.

v · Rain

Rain, gathered

Rain gathered into drops, falling on tuned glass.

Rain alone on glass is noise. Gathered, then released from a height, each drop strikes tuned glass in the open skywell. Light rain touches the octave and fifth. Heavier rain reaches the third, sixth, seventh and ninth, then the eleventh and thirteenth.

Show

Each glass hangs at its own height. Drops shaken loose together land in order of their fall, lower notes first, so an interval opens over a fraction of a second. A gathered drop carries the energy of roughly fifteen raindrops. Drainage keeps the glass at pitch.

vi · Light

Light

A blade of sun marks the hour and bends the pitch.

Sealed slits on the south face. A blade of sun moves across the wall with the hour, and at noon its reach marks the season. Its heat bends the pedal, so the sun is also heard.

Figures

A 5 mm slit casts a blade that travels about 1.3 m per hour across a wall 5 m away. At 35° N, noon light reaches 0.6 m into the room at midsummer, 4.9 m at midwinter.

vii · Stillness

Stillness

Windless days are silent.

On windless, dry days the building is silent. Silence belongs to the weather.

ix · Method

Measured, inherited, chosen, hoped

Every claim carries one label.

Each claim carries one label: measured, inherited, chosen, or hoped. The trigrams give the building its order. They make no claim about nature.

Open questions
  1. H1. Is tuned wind heard as more pleasant than untuned wind?
  2. H2. Does the spacing rule predict how rough a mix sounds?
  3. H3. Is the slow sun beat heard as calm, or as out of tune?

Roughness follows the Plomp–Levelt model. It measures one aspect of hearing, not musical meaning.

Principles
  1. Silence is shared weather.
  2. No health or therapeutic claims. Listeners are never sensed.
  3. Nature plays. The building tunes.
  4. One pedal, many colours.
  5. Spacing before scale.
  6. Each opening serves sound and light where it can.
