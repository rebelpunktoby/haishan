Constitution山海憲章 · ten articles
  1. Time initiates every sound and sets the question, never the answer.
  2. The body-channel reading is a poetic lens for attention, never a claim about your body.
  3. The music takes a stance — 扶 定 疏 柔 動 安 — and embrace, which changes nothing, is always available.
  4. Silence is speech. Harmony is balanced relationship. Everything is relational material.
  5. Reflection comes first. Any settling is its possible consequence, never the target.
  6. No layer reaches past its neighbor. Every boundary is an interpretation with latitude.
  7. OceanMountain makes no medical claim. It treats, balances and heals nothing. The listener completes the work.
  8. No number — brainwave, tuning, “organ frequency” — is an emotional or medical truth.
  9. Every mapping from cosmology to sound rests on a shared relational quality and stays open to revision.
  10. Instruments, synthesis and exact sounds may change without limit. This constitution may not.
