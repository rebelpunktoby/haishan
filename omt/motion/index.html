About

OMT Motion is the third part of OceanMountain. The Engine (聲境) plays the hour; the Site (殿境) lets weather play a building; Motion (身境) lets a body play the room.

A camera reads how much she moves, how fast, how high, where, and how still. Those readings never play notes directly. They push a few slow forces (energy, brightness, density, chaos, stillness), and the instrument decides what to say: when to open, when to strike, when to storm, when to rest. That gap between the body and the sound is the point: it is meant to answer like a partner, not follow like a shadow.

Four worlds

土GroundLow harmonics, slow, close to the fundamental.
水WaterMiddle partials that drift and beat against each other.
火FireThe 7th, 11th and 13th: bright, unstable, quick to strike.
空AirHigh, thin, far away; the space above the others.

All four are drawn from one harmonic series on A, or from the resonances of your own room if you measure it in the Rig. The four are a design choice, not the Five Phases (五行).

Four registers

OceanMountain keeps every claim in one of four registers, so that what is measured is never confused with what is chosen or hoped.

Measured可量測Motion, speed, height, position and stillness from the camera; pitch and attacks from the microphone; room resonances from a ten-second test sweep.
Traditional-symbolic傳統象徵None claimed. The glyphs 土 水 火 空 are names for the worlds, not a cosmological mapping.
Design choice藝術設計The four worlds and their harmonic ladders; the breath pace (about six a minute); the rule modes; how chaos is earned, spent and gated by layering.
Hypothesis實驗假設That the quality of movement (flow, time, weight, space), read from a flat image, can shape musical form, not only loudness. Untested.

No health or therapeutic claims are made. The breath pace is a musical decision.

Privacy

Camera and microphone are processed inside your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. A stage area and room tuning, if you set them, are saved only in this browser. Pose tracking, when you turn it on, downloads its model from Google's servers.

Status

Beta 0.20, made in November 2026 for a live piano and contemporary dance improvisation in Mexico City. Next: a shared generator with the Engine, and a sound engine in Max/RNBO. Touch mode is the gentlest start; camera mode is the real instrument.

Her body

  • Moving opens the upper partials of the world and makes your remembered notes ring.
  • Reaching up or going to the floor tilts the bed and lifts or drops the remembered notes an octave.
  • Left or right moves the image across the speaker.
  • A sudden change strikes one pure tone from the world or from your notes. It is a single bell, never a burst.
  • Wild motion past the Storm threshold brings in the Storm: noise tuned to the active worlds, FM grit and stutter. It leaves about a second after she calms.
  • Holding still lets the room tail swell. With advance on, 8 seconds moves to the next world. If several worlds are layered, it drops the oldest one instead; Air stays.

Your piano

  • Each clear attack is heard and added to the memory, up to six notes. The oldest drops out.
  • The memory layer only holds piano sound. When you stop, it keeps your last phrase, not the room.
  • Answer mode steps back while you play and comes forward when you rest.
  • Snap to A series pulls remembered notes to the nearest harmonic of A, so they sit inside the bed instead of against it.
