山海 · OceanMountain

A generative sound work,
shaped by the hour and the season.
The solar terms form its ground;
the double‑hours, its tide.
Nothing is composed in advance.
It proceeds one step at a time,
retaining only what has truly sounded.

一件隨時辰與節氣而生的聲音作品。
以二十四節氣為地，
以十二時辰為潮。
無預先寫定之樂譜，
音樂一步一步而行，
唯留真正響起之聲。

breath · tide · hour · season
